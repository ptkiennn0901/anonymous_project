# Paired X-ray image--report samples

`paired_sample_reports.json` supplies one Vietnamese report for each of `xray_sample_004` through `xray_sample_006`. The reports are paired with their matching images and contain only the repository-local sample IDs.

The release remains conditional on manual privacy review of both image pixels and report text. Source identifiers, dates, PACS links, service names, and original filenames are excluded.
